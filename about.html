Professional Journey

My journey into cybersecurity began with curiosity about how systems operate, fail, and defend themselves under real-world conditions. What started as exploring Linux environments and security tools gradually evolved into building practical projects focused on detection engineering, web security, reconnaissance automation, and secure software development. Over time, I began documenting hands-on learning through technical writeups, cybersecurity research, and tool-focused tutorials covering areas such as penetration testing, WordPress security, enumeration, and vulnerability assessment. This platform serves as a growing archive of experiments, walkthroughs, and cybersecurity concepts explored through practical application. My interests primarily revolve around offensive security, adversarial research, OSINT, detection systems, and security-focused engineering. I strongly believe that understanding how attacks work is essential to building stronger defensive systems. This blog is a space dedicated to continuous learning, experimentation, and sharing knowledge with the cybersecurity community through clean documentation, practical demonstrations, and project-driven exploration.
